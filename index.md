# MIND Laboratory

## Mathematics for Intelligent Networks & Decisions Laboratory

Welcome to the **MIND Lab** in the department of artificial intelligence at the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad. We develop mathematical foundations and computational methods for intelligent, networked, and decision-making systems.

Our work lies at the intersection of **multi-agent systems, control theory, stochastic systems, formal methods, and trustworthy artificial intelligence**.

---

## Research

### Multi-Agent Systems

Distributed coordination, consensus, resilient networks, secure multi-agent systems, formation control, and distributed decision-making.

[Explore Multi-Agent Systems →](/research/multi-agent-systems/)

### Stochastic Systems

Probabilistic robustness, uncertainty quantification, stochastic control, and mathematical distances between dynamical systems.

[Explore Stochastic Systems →](/research/stochastic-systems/)

### Formal Methods

Temporal logic, epistemic logic, formal verification, and specification-driven planning for intelligent and autonomous systems.

[Explore Formal Methods →](/research/formal-methods/)

### Verifiable AI

Mathematical tools for trustworthy, safe, interpretable, and formally verifiable artificial intelligence.

[Explore Verifiable AI →](/research/verifiable-ai/)

## MIND Philosophy

We are interested in the mathematical principles that allow intelligent agents to **reason, coordinate, decide, and act reliably** in uncertain and networked environments.

The lab brings together ideas from:

- Control and dynamical systems
- Artificial intelligence
- Probability and stochastic systems
- Optimization
- Graph theory
- Formal methods
- Multi-agent systems
