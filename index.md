# MIND Laboratory

## Welcome to the Mathematics for Intelligent Networks & Decisions Laboratory (**MIND Lab**) in the department of artificial intelligence at the IIT Hyderabad. 


We develop mathematical foundations and computational methods for intelligent, and networked systems. Our work lies at the intersection of **multi-agent systems, control theory, stochastic processes, formal methods, and risk-aware artificial intelligence**.

---

## MIND Lab Philosophy

We are interested in the mathematical principles that allow intelligent networked systems to **reason, coordinate, decide, and act reliably** in uncertain and networked environments. The lab brings together ideas from Control and dynamical systems, Artificial intelligence, Probability and stochastic systems, Optimization, Graph theory, and Formal methods to address open problems in research literature.
