# MIND Laboratory

## Welcome to the Mathematics for Intelligent Networks & Decisions Laboratory (**MIND Lab**) in the department of artificial intelligence at the IIT Hyderabad. 


We develop mathematical foundations and computational methods for intelligent, networked, and decision-making systems. Our work lies at the intersection of **multi-agent systems, control theory, stochastic systems, formal methods, and trustworthy artificial intelligence**.

---

## MIND Lab Philosophy

We are interested in the mathematical principles that allow intelligent networked systems to **reason, coordinate, decide, and act reliably** in uncertain and networked environments. The lab brings together ideas from:

- Control and dynamical systems
- Artificial intelligence
- Probability and stochastic systems
- Optimization
- Graph theory
- Formal methods
- Multi-agent systems
